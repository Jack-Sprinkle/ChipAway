# Chip Away Golf Vision

> **Version:** 0.1
> **Status:** Living Document
> **Last Updated:** June 2026

---

# Why We're Building This

Chip Away Golf isn't being built to become another golf content channel.

It's being built to become a place golfers trust.

The goal isn't to chase algorithms, publish endless swing tips, or convince people there's one secret to playing better golf.

The goal is to create products, content, and tools that make golfers excited to play again.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Imagine a golfer discovering Chip Away Golf for the first time.

They might watch a YouTube video.

Then read a blog article.

Then use the scorekeeper for their next round.

Then subscribe to the newsletter.

Months later, they're still using Chip Away because it has become part of how they experience golf.

Every interaction should reinforce one simple idea:

Golf is something to enjoy for a lifetime with friends and family.

---

# What Success Looks Like

Success is not measured solely by views, followers, or revenue.

Success means:

* People spend more time enjoying golf.
* Golfers become more patient with themselves.
* The software solves real problems.
* The content feels genuine.
* The community is welcoming and encouraging.
* Every product reflects the same philosophy.

If Chip Away disappeared tomorrow, people would genuinely miss it.

---

# What We Will Build

## Software

Useful tools that make playing and practicing golf easier.

Examples include:

* Scorekeeper
* Round statistics
* Practice journal
* Handicap tools
* Course notes
* Future ideas that genuinely help golfers.

---

## Content

Honest stories about golf.

Not because we're the best golfer.

Because we're willing to share the journey.

Content includes:

* YouTube
* Blog
* Newsletter
* Podcasts (future)
* Short-form video

---

## Products

Simple products golfers actually enjoy using.

Not products for the sake of having merchandise.

Every product should solve a problem or reinforce the Chip Away philosophy.

---

# The Community We Hope to Build

We want Chip Away to feel like playing nine holes with a good friend.

Supportive.

Curious.

Relaxed.

Welcoming.

Never intimidating.

---

# Design Philosophy

Everything we create should feel:

* Clean
* Intentional
* Calm
* Modern
* Timeless

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

Avoid visual noise.

Design should quietly support the experience rather than compete for attention.

---

# Decision Framework

Whenever we're unsure about a feature, product, or piece of content, ask:

1. Does it genuinely help golfers?
2. Is it authentic to who we are?
3. Would we personally enjoy using it?
4. Does it make the game feel more enjoyable?
5. Does it fit our long-term vision?

If the answer to any of these is "no," rethink it.

---

# Five-Year Vision

Chip Away Golf is recognized as a thoughtful golf brand built around progress, enjoyment, and useful tools.

People don't just consume the content.

They participate.

They use the software.

They share their own journeys.

They recommend Chip Away because it genuinely improves their experience of the game.

# Our Promise

We'll never promise a perfect golf swing.

One shot at a time.

Play. Breathe. Enjoy.
