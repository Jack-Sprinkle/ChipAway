> **Version:** 0.2
> **Status:** Living Document
> **Last Updated:** June 2026

# Purpose

Chip Away Golf exists to help golfers improve their game without losing sight of why they started playing.

Golf is difficult, frustrating, and often humbling. It's easy to become consumed by scorecards, swing thoughts, and expectations. Chip Away Golf encourages a different approach: consistent improvement through patience, curiosity, and enjoyment.

This isn't a brand about perfect golf.

It's about building a lifelong relationship with the game.

---

# Mission

Help golfers enjoy the game more while improving one shot at a time.

---

# Brand Promise

We won't promise overnight improvement.

We won't chase clickbait.

We'll build thoughtful content, useful software, and authentic experiences that make golfers excited to tee it up again.

---

# Core Principles

## Progress Over Perfection

Improvement happens gradually.

---

## Build Useful Things

Software, articles, videos, and products should all solve real problems.

---

## Teach Through Experience

Document the journey rather than pretending to have every answer.

## Stay Authentic

## Simplicity Wins

# Brand Personality

Chip Away Golf is:

* Calm
* Honest
* Thoughtful
* Encouraging
* Curious
* Practical
* Humble

Chip Away Golf is **not**:

* Loud
* Flashy
* Ego-driven
* Clickbait
* Elitist
* Overcomplicated

---

# Visual Philosophy

Chip Away Golf should feel like walking nine holes on a quiet morning.

Natural.

Minimal.

Warm.

Timeless.

Design should never compete with the content.

Whitespace is intentional.

Animations should be subtle.

Photography should feel authentic rather than staged.

Avoid visual clutter.

## Logo Rules

Always:

* Maintain generous whitespace.
* Preserve aspect ratio.
* Use approved brand colors.

Never:

* Stretch or distort.
* Rotate.
* Add shadows or effects.
* Change colors.
* Place on busy backgrounds.

# Photography Style

Photography should feel authentic.

Preferred imagery:

* Sunrise tee times
* Walking golfers
* Golf bags on the fairway
* Dew-covered greens
* Coffee before a round
* Practice greens
* Natural light
* Real golfers enjoying the game

Avoid:

* Oversaturated colors
* Heavy editing
* Artificial poses
* Excessive action shots
* Visual clutter

---

# Tone of Voice

Write like you're talking with a playing partner after the round.

Be:

* Encouraging
* Honest
* Relaxed
* Helpful
* Curious

Avoid:

* Fear-based headlines
* "Secret swing tip" language
* Arrogance
* Exaggeration

---

# Design Principles

* Mobile-first
* Accessibility-first
* Minimal animations
* Consistent spacing
* Rounded corners
* Calm color palette
* High readability
* Fast loading

---

# Brand Test

Before publishing any content, shipping a feature, or designing a product, ask:

* Does this genuinely help golfers?
* Does it encourage progress rather than perfection?
* Does it feel authentic?
* Would it feel at home on a peaceful morning walking nine holes?
* Does it reinforce **Play. Breathe. Enjoy.**

If the answer is "yes," it belongs in Chip Away Golf.
